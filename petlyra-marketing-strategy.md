### Winning Formula (Apply to All Petlyra Ads)

1. Lead with pet owner GUILT or FEAR — not features
2. Visual contrast > visual beauty (hot vs cool, before vs after)
3. Specific numbers beat adjectives
4. One bold claim per ad — never stack 5 benefits
5. Pet owners buy with their HEART
6. Risk reversal closes the sale
7. Anti-ad aesthetic wins on Meta

## 🔍 Strategic Diagnosis

### The Real Funnel (3-day Shopify data, pre-new-website)
| Stage | Count | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | 163 | — |
| Added to cart | 12 | 7.36% ✅ above industry avg |
| Reached checkout | 6 | **3.68% — 50% LEAK** 🔴 |
| Completed | 5 | 3.07% ✅ above industry avg |

**Diagnosis:** Cart-to-checkout drop of 50% is the single biggest leak. Not the ads — the cart page.

### Identified Issues (Pre-New-Website)
1. ❌ Buy 1 and Buy 1 Get 1 Free both displayed as $64.99 (confusing pricing)
2. ❌ "Buy 1" was default-selected (should be BOGO)
3. ❌ Size-bundle mismatch suspected at L/XL/XXL pricing
4. ❌ Customers removing free items from cart (UpCart UX issue)
5. ❌ No abandoned cart email/SMS flow

### Confirmed Winners
- **Ad 3** (Panting Question — MOF Emotion): 75% close rate at peak — sharpest closer
- **Ad 2** (BOGO Hero / Two Mats One Price — MOF BOF): Best ATC machine, $2.47 CPC link
- New website (launched May 18) showing early signs of fixing BOF close rate (20% → 33%)

### Confirmed Dead Angles
- Any "Trust" / logical / comparison-only creative for cold MOF audiences
- TOF cold acquisition with non-emotional hooks

## ⚙️ Operating Framework

- **1 campaign, 3 ad sets, $100/day CBO** (~$33/ad set effective)
- **Decision unit:** Ad set primarily, individual ad if losing ad in winning set
- **48-hour kill rule:**
  - Losing ad set after 48h → kill entirely
  - Losing ad within winning set → kill that specific ad
- **MOF Trust permanently retired** (confirmed dead across 2 campaigns)

### Honest tradeoff to know
At $33/ad set/day with €75 CPA, 48h = less than 1 conversion event = statistically thin. Rule is fine for survival; tighten to 72h once budget scales above $200/day.

## 📌 Permanent Rules / Learnings

1. **Never test "Trust" / logical-only angles** for cold MOF traffic on pet products — emotion wins
2. **48h kill rule** applies at ad set level; ad-level kill OK for losing ads in winning sets
3. **Always use real product photos as image-to-image reference** — never text-only generation for ads
4. **No "DIE" / "death" / "kill" wording** in ad copy or images — Meta policy risk
5. **Default-select the highest-AOV bundle option** (BOGO over Buy 1)
6. **Never change two variables (ads + funnel) in the same 24h window** — kills diagnostic clarity
7. **Cumulative ROAS < BER means decisions, not panic** — examine WHY before killing
8. **Brand template consistency in BOF** (blue/cream split + Petlyra wordmark + offer block)

---

## 🚫 What NOT To Do

- ❌ Don't kill the campaign without diagnosis (Day 4 was bad but for clear reasons)
- ❌ Don't 2× the budget while CPA is unstable
- ❌ Don't refresh winning ads out of panic — replace alongside, don't pause
- ❌ Don't add 5 new ad sets at once — pixel fragmentation
- ❌ Don't use "Limited time only" without a real deadline (trust erosion)
- ❌ Don't fake review counts — use real numbers even if smaller
