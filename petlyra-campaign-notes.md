## 1. Product & Offer

**Brand:** Petlyra
**Product:** Pet cooling mat
**Site:** https://petlyra.com/products/cooling-mat
**SKU details:**
- Colors: Blue (hero), Pink, Gray, Brown
- Sizes: S, M, L, XL, XXL
- Price varies by size (S = $64.99, XXL = ~$132)

**Offer structure (BOGO format):**
- Buy 1 Get 1 Free (50% off per mat — best per-unit deal)
- Buy 2 Get 1 Free — labeled "Most Popular" (33% off per mat — worst per-unit deal)
- Buy 3 Get 2 Free — labeled "Best Value" (40% off per mat)
- Customer pays based on most expensive size selected
- Free bonuses: Free shipping, Hair Removal Glove, Cleaning Wipes
- Brand line: *"Because your dog deserves a little extra love ❤️"*

**Known pricing math issue:** BOGO is the cheapest per-mat deal, making the "Most Popular" Buy 2 Get 1 the worst per-unit price. The labels are doing emotional work that current AOV (€121) depends on. At scale, savvy buyers may downgrade to BOGO and compress AOV.

**COGS:** ~€30.36 per unit. At BOGO ($64.99 ≈ €60), unit economics are very tight (loss before ad spend).

## 2. Audience Insights (from data)

**Demographic:** 71% of converting spend in Campaign 1 went to **age 65+**. All 3 first-campaign purchases came from this segment. Meta's algorithm found this audience without manual targeting.

**Targeting decision (Campaign 2):** 45–65+ (includes 45–54 as a hedge — undertested, not definitively a non-converter).

**Audience-creative match:** 55+/65+ buyers respond to:
- ✅ Emotion (panting, overheating, "suffering")
- ✅ Direct offers (BOGO, money-back guarantee)
- ✅ Pattern interrupts (thermal vision, question hooks)
- ✅ Authority signals (vet endorsement, media logos)
- ❌ Pure logical/feature/comparison ads (MOF Trust failed in 3 campaigns)

## 3. Funnel Diagnostics

**Latest 8-day Shopify funnel:**
| Stage | Conversion | Industry avg (pet) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sessions → ATC | 11.21% | 4–7% | ✅ Excellent (ads working) |
| ATC → Reached Checkout | 32.07% | 50–60% | 🔴 **Biggest leak** |
| Checkout → Completed | 58.82% | 70–80% | 🟡 Below average |
| Overall conversion | 2.11% | 2–3% | 🟡 Below average |

**Key insight:** The leak is at the **cart page**, not the checkout itself. People add to cart, see the bundle/total, and bail before starting checkout. Fixing this single leak would boost ROAS by ~50% with zero extra ad spend.

**Suspected causes:**
- Bundle complexity (3 tiers, hard math)
- 80% off discount triggers "too good to be true"
- Fake countdown timer (resets on reload)
- "Most Popular" tag conflicts with actual best-value math

## 7. House Rules for All Prompts

These are locked rules established during the session:

1. **No `[REPLACE]` markers ever** — always use safe defaults like `"Lasts all summer"`, `"Trusted by thousands of pet parents"`, `"Limited Stock Available"`
2. **Pet signal mandatory** — every ad must have at least one of:
   - Dog/cat visible in image
   - Word "dog" or "pet" in headline
   - Paw icon clearly readable (not just in tiny brand mark)
3. **Brand mark required** — PETLYRA wordmark + paw icon in every ad
4. **Reference image: YES** — cooling mat photo must be attached as reference for every prompt; mat must match exact reference (color #7BB7D6, oval-pebble quilted pattern, piped edges, rectangular plush)
5. **Avoid Spark Paws tagline** — never use "Less Panting. More Chilling." (tagline collision risk)
6. **Single image color per ad** — consistency within one image (don't mix mat colors unless it's the multi-dog grid showing variety)

## 8. Campaign-Level Operating Rules

**Persistent campaign architecture:**
- Keep ONE campaign alive long-term
- Swap ad sets in/out as needed
- Plan to launch a fresh campaign every 8–12 weeks (when fatigue hits, or for new audience tests)

**When to pause an ad set:**
| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 ATCs after $30+ spent + 2 days | Pause |
| ROAS < 1.0 average for 3+ days | Pause |
| Link CTR < 1.5% + CPC link > $7 | Pause |

**When NOT to disrupt:**
- During the first 48 hours of Learning
- When making multiple changes at once (do one at a time, watch 48h, then next)
- When the campaign just turned profitable (let momentum build)

**Budget rules:**
- Don't lower budget during a working trajectory (signals "stop" to the algo)
- Wait 5–7 days of stable ROAS before scaling up
- New ad set inside existing campaign = no Learning reset for other ad sets
- New campaign = full Learning reset, all signal starts from zero
