## 4. Key Learnings from Campaign 1

### What Worked
- **Fear + numbers + clean backgrounds** beat soft sentimental hooks in this test (winners: Ad 1 financial fear, Ad 9 health fear)
- **However, the soft emotional ads never got a fair test** — CBO starved them ($1-4/day) before they could convert. Cannot conclude emotion lost.
- **9.5% real conversion rate** proves the funnel works — bottleneck is traffic quality, not checkout
- **Buyers convert late** (2 AM pattern confirmed). Don't judge a day before night ends.
- **High AOV products sell** — XXL ($132) and L ($99.99) both converted

### What Hurt
- **Scaling too early** (20% on Day 2) broke learning and reallocated budget badly
- **Too many ads in CBO** caused budget dilution (11 of 13 ads lost money)
- **CBO over-concentrated** (66% into Ad 9 on Day 3, starving Ad 1)
- **High CPMs** ($77-157) made every click expensive

### Process Lessons
- One change at a time during learning phase (don't kill AND scale simultaneously)
- Real conversion rate must be bot-adjusted (~30% of sessions were bots)
- Daily attribution can lag — count late-night/2 AM sales before cutting
- Discipline rules (48h, money or off) protect against panic moves

## 6. Decision Framework / Kill Rules

### Per Ad Set (48h Rule)
| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Ad set produces 0 sales in 48h with meaningful spend | **Kill that ad set** |
| Ad set above 2.0 ROAS for 3 days | **Scale 20%** (then wait 72h before scaling again) |
| Blended campaign ROAS < 1.0 after 4 clean days | Restructure or pause |

### Per Day
| Blended ROAS by Day 3-4 | Verdict |
|---|---|
| > 1.5 | Working — scale 20% |
| 1.3 – 1.5 | Profitable — kill losers, keep optimizing |
| 1.0 – 1.3 | Marginal — restructure around winning angle |
| < 1.0 | Stop, rethink offer/audience |

### Anti-Panic Rules
- Don't judge before 48h on a new ad set
- Don't scale AND kill on the same day
- Don't cut at hour 24 when the rule is 48h
- Always count late-night sales before deciding
- BOF retargeting needs a pool of ~1,000+ first — don't kill it for low volume

## 8. Reference — Anti-AI Image Rules

Every image prompt must include:
- Real product photography or graphic-design layout (typography + clean product shot)
- No AI-rendered dogs, people, or lifestyle rooms
- 2 mats shown for BOGO (not 1, not 3)
- "BUY 1 GET 1 FREE + FREE GIFT" offer
- "90-day cool-or-refund guarantee" (never 30-day)
- 4 color swatches at bottom (blue, pink, beige, gray)
- No fake "as seen in" media logos
- No ALL CAPS in primary text
- Max 3 emojis per ad
- Lead with pain (first line shows before "See more")

Safe visual styles:
- Clean product hero + bold pain headline
- Thermal before/after split
- Sticky-note UGC flat lay (wood table)
- iPhone review screenshot
- Real lifestyle photos (verified ownership)
- Comparison chart (X / ✓ columns)
